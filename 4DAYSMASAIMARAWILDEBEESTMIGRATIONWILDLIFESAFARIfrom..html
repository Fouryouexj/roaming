4 DAYS MASAI MARA WILDEBEEST MIGRATION WILDLIFE SAFARI

Embark on our Deluxe Roaming Nomads Tour Package for a thrilling journey through Kenya's iconic national parks. Encounter unique wildlife, enjoy luxurious accommodations, and embark on exhilarating game drives, all meticulously arranged for your comfort and enjoyment. With inclusions like park fees, transport, accommodation, meals, and expert guides, this package guarantees an unforgettable African experience.

Unleashing the Thrill

Experience the awe-inspiring wonder of nature with Roaming Nomads as we delve into the mesmerizing Masai Mara wildebeest migration. Every year, this captivating spectacle draws thousands of tourists to Kenya, eager to witness the breathtaking journey of over 1.5 million wildebeest, zebras, and gazelles.
Embark on an unforgettable journey as we explore the intricacies of the Masai Mara wildebeest migration safari.
This remarkable annual event unfolds between the Masai Mara National Reserve in Kenya and the Serengeti National Park in Tanzania, spanning a circular route that spans over 1,800 miles.It’s hailed as one of the most remarkable wildlife phenomena on the planet.

Driven by the pursuit of greener pastures and water sources, the migration kicks off as the dry season takes hold. Witness the awe-inspiring sight of wildebeest, zebras, and gazelles as they journey from the Serengeti in Tanzania to the lush plains of the Masai Mara in Kenya. However, their path is fraught with peril, from navigating crocodile-infested rivers to evading the relentless predators such as lions, cheetahs, and hyenas.

Day 1: Nairobi – Masai Mara Game Reserve.

Start your adventure with Roaming Nomads as our driver and staff pick you up from the airport or your hotel in the morning.
After a brief safari briefing, we depart Nairobi, heading through the scenic views of the Great Rift Valley escarpment.Take a stopover at the Great Rift Valley viewpoint for photos and shopping before continuing the safari to Masai Mara Game Reserve. Arrive in time for lunch at a budget camp or luxury lodge. After lunch, relax before embarking on an evening game drive until 6.30 pm.

Masai Mara Game Reserve is renowned for the Big Five – Leopard, Lion, Rhino, Elephant, and Buffalo, and is home to numerous other African wildlife species. Enjoy dinner and overnight stay at the luxury lodge.

Day 2: Full Day Roaming Nomads Tour in Masai Mara Game Reserve.

Rise early for a full breakfast before venturing into the park for a day filled with adventure. Masai Mara Game Reserve boasts a high concentration of wildlife, promising an exhilarating experience with Roaming Nomads.
Enjoy a picnic lunch, and opt for an optional visit to a Masai Village to witness traditional dancing and singing by the Masai Warriors and women. Get a glimpse into their way of life and capture memorable photos.
Dinner and overnight stay at the luxury lodge.

Day 3: Full Day Roaming Nomads Tour in Masai Mara Game Reserve

Another thrilling day awaits with Roaming Nomads. Start with an early morning breakfast before setting out for more wildlife viewing in the park.
The rich biodiversity of Masai Mara Game Reserve ensures an amazing and joyful adventure. Enjoy a picnic lunch amidst nature’s splendor.
Dinner and overnight stay at the luxury lodge.

Day 4: Masai Mara Game Reserve – Nairobi City

On the fourth day of your Roaming Nomads Tour, wake up early for a morning game drive within Masai Mara Game Reserve. After the game drive, return to the luxury lodge for a full breakfast before departing for Nairobi by road. Lunch will be served en route,and you’ll arrive in Nairobi in the afternoon.
Our driver guide will drop you off at your hotel or the airport, marking the end of your safari filled with unforgettable memories from Roaming Nomads.

Witnessing the Masai Mara wildebeest migration safari in Kenya is an extraordinary experience, witnessing over 1.5 million animals moving in unison. It’s not just about the wildebeest, zebras, and gazelles, but also the predators that follow them, creating a raw and emotional experience that underscores the circle of life in the animal kingdom.
