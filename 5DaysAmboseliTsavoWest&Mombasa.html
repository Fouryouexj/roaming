5 Days Amboseli, Tsavo West & Mombasa

Enjoy a final game drive in Amboseli after breakfast. Return to the lodge for lunch and check-out. Depart for Nairobi in the afternoon. Arrive in Nairobi, and depending on your preference, you can be dropped off at your hotel or the airport.

Best of Kenyan Safari

Day 1: Nairobi – Maasai Mara Game Reserve

Depart from Nairobi in the morning.
Drive to the Maasai Mara Game Reserve, passing through the Great Rift Valley along the way.
Arrive at the Maasai Mara and check-in at your chosen accommodation.
Enjoy a delicious dinner and relax at the camp/lodge.

Day 2: Full Day Maasai Mara Game Drive

Start your day with an early morning game drive to witness the wildlife awakening.
Return to the camp/lodge for breakfast.
Spend the rest of the day exploring the Maasai Mara and its remarkable wildlife.
Picnic lunch in the park.
Evening game drive and return to your accommodation for dinner and relaxation.

Day 3: Maasai Mara – Lake Naivasha

After breakfast, depart from the Maasai Mara.
Drive to Lake Naivasha, known for its stunning scenery and diverse birdlife
Check-in at your chosen accommodation.
Take a boat ride on Lake Naivasha to see hippos and various bird species.
Dinner and overnight stay at your lodge or camp.

Day 4: Lake Naivasha – Lake Nakuru National

Enjoy breakfast and depart for Lake Nakuru National Park.
Arrive at Lake Nakuru and embark on a game drive to spot flamingos, rhinos, lions, and more.
Lunch at your chosen accommodation.
Afternoon game drive in Lake Nakuru.
Dinner and overnight stay in the park.

Day 5: Lake Nakuru – Amboseli National Park

Have breakfast and depart for Amboseli National Park.
Enjoy scenic views as you drive towards the park, which is known for its majestic Mount Kilimanjaro backdrop
Arrive in Amboseli, check in, and have lunch.
Evening game drive in Amboseli, with opportunities to see elephants, lions, cheetahs, and other wildlife.
Dinner and overnight stay at your chosen camp or lodge.

Day 6: Full Day in Amboseli

Begin your day with a sunrise game drive to capture the beauty of Mount Kilimanjaro.
Return to the camp/lodge for breakfast.
Spend the day exploring Amboseli, with both morning and afternoon game drives.
Picnic lunch in the park.
Dinner and relaxation at your accommodation.

Day 7: Amboseli – Nairobi

Enjoy a final game drive in Amboseli after breakfast.
Return to the lodge for lunch and check-out.
Depart for Nairobi in the afternoon.
Arrive in Nairobi, and depending on your preference, you can be dropped off at your hotel or the airport.
