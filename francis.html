About us

We are Kenya's foremost and
most reliable travel agency.

Roaming Nomad Tours and Travels Limited is a comprehensive Tours & Travel company dedicated to providing all the information and assistance you need to uncover the stunning beauty of Kenya through our meticulously planned itineraries. We are your ultimate destination for travel information, advice, and the latest tourism trends that inspire you to travel or even dream about your next adventure. We promise you the finest safari experience to the top destinations, including the renowned Masai Mara, famous for the great wildebeest migration, as well as Samburu, Amboseli, Tsavo, Lake Nakuru, and many more.
