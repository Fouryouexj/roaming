Your Adventure Questions Answered

Get safari-ready with essential info for your Roaming Nomads journey!

When should I book my dream safari?
For prime wildlife viewing seasons, secure your spot 6-12 months ahead. But never worry - we'll move mountains to accommodate last-minute explorers when possible!
What amazing creatures will I encounter?
Prepare for heart-racing moments with the legendary Big Five, graceful giraffes, striped zebra parades, and speedy cheetahs. With luck, you might spot rare mountain gorillas or playful lemurs!
Is this safari kid-friendly?
Absolutely! Our family safaris turn little ones into junior rangers with interactive tracking adventures, wildlife quizzes, and special child-friendly accommodations.
What's in my adventure toolkit?
Think safari-chic: neutral-toned clothing, comfy trail shoes, sun armor (hat + SPF 50+), zoom-tastic binoculars, and camera gear to make your friends back home jealous!
Can you handle my travel logistics?
From flight magic to luxury tented camps, we've got you covered! Our travel wizards handle every detail so you can focus on spotting leopards in acacia trees.
