    • 5 Days Amboseli, Tsavo West & Mombasa

    This 5-day private safari showcases the best of Kenya's wildlife and landscapes, from the elephants of Amboseli to the diverse wildlife of Tsavo West, and the coastal allure of Mombasa. Roaming Nomads ensures your journey is both exciting and luxurious, offering private guides, comfortable accommodations, and unforgettable experiences.

    Wildlife in Amboseli and Tsavo West

    ​✅Roaming Nomads: 5-Day Private Safari – Amboseli, Tsavo West, and Mombasa
    Duration: 5 Days Destinations: Amboseli National Park, Tsavo West National Park, and Mombasa.
    Experience the diversity of Kenya’s wildlife and landscapes on a captivating private safari with Roaming Nomads.

    Day 1: Nairobi to Amboseli National Park

    Morning:7:00 AM:

    ✅Roaming Nomads guide will pick you up from your Nairobi hotel.
    ✅Begin your journey to Amboseli National Park, a place where elephants reign.

    Late Morning:11:00 AM:

    ✅Arrive in Amboseli and check in at your selected lodge or camp.
    ✅Savor a delectable lunch overlooking the magnificent Kilimanjaro.
    ✅Set out on an afternoon game drive to witness the park’s wildlife, including elephants, lions, and cheetahs.
    ✅Return to your accommodation for dinner and relaxation.

    Day 2: Amboseli to Tsavo West National Park

    Morning:6:30 AM:

    ✅Wake up to a striking Amboseli sunrise and enjoy breakfast.
    ✅Embark on a morning game drive, capturing the beauty of Kilimanjaro as the backdrop.

    Mid-Morning:10:30 AM:

    ✅Depart for Tsavo West National Park, known for its diverse wildlife and breathtaking landscapes.

    Afternoon:1:00 PM:

    ✅Check in at your chosen luxury lodge or camp in Tsavo West.
    ✅Enjoy a delightful lunch and some downtime before your next adventure.
    ✅Take an afternoon game drive in Tsavo West to spot wildlife like hippos, crocodiles, and a variety of birds.
    ✅Dinner and overnight stay in Tsavo West.

    Day 3: Half Day in Tsavo West National Park then to Mombasa

    Morning:6:00 AM:

    ✅Begin your day with a sunrise game drive to capture Tsavo’s early wildlife activity.
    ✅Return to your lodge for breakfast.

    Mid-Morning:

    ✅Visit the remarkable Mzima Springs, home to hippos and crocodiles, and the Shetani Lava Flows for a geological marvel. Explore the park with game drives, where you may encounter leopards, buffaloes, and diverse antelope species.

    Afternoon:1:00 PM:

    ✅Enjoy a sumptuous lunch at your lodge.

    2.00 PM:

    ✅Depart Tsavo West for the coastal paradise of Mombasa. Arrive in Mombasa, check in at your selected beachfront resort, and enjoy a coastal-style dinner.
    ✅Dinner and relaxation at your accommodation.

    Day 4: Full Day in Mombasa

    Morning:10:30 AM:

    ✅Spend your afternoon at leisure, relaxing on the beach, taking part in water sports, or exploring Mombasa’s cultural and historical sites.

    Day 5: Departure from Mombasa

    Morning:

    ✅Enjoy a leisurely morning on the beautiful Mombasa coast.
    ✅Check out from your resort and transfer to Mombasa Airport or your chosen departure point.
