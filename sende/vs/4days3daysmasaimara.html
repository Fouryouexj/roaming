    • 4 Days 3 Nights Masai Mara Budget Safari - $590 PP

    The Nairobi to Masai Mara safari package includes a 4-day trip with game drives, accommodation at your camp, and meals. Travelers will explore the Masai Mara Game Reserve, known for its connection to the Serengeti, and enjoy full-day excursions with picnic lunches. The package covers park fees, transport, meals, and guide services but excludes tips, personal items, and unspecified expenses.

    Day 1 Nairobi – Masai Mara

    ✅After breakfast Pick up from your hotel around 7.00am join other travelers and depart Nairobi to Maasai Mara Game Reserve with a stop over at the great rift valley view point.
    ✅You arrive masai mara late afternoon, early evening and proceed for a game drive dinner and overnight at your camp.

    Day 2 -Masai mara full day

    ✅After breakfast depart with picnic lunch boxes, you’ll spent full day exploring this unique park which really forms the northern part of the famous Serengeti National Park.
    ✅Meals and overnight at your camp.

    Day 3.Fullday in Masai mara

    ✅After early breakfast get your picnic lunch boxes and head for another fullday game drives returning back in the evening for dinner and overnight at your camp.

    Day 4: Masai Mara – Nairobi

    ✅After breakfast head back to Nairobi with lunch enroute arriving back 3pm getting dropped back at your hotel/airport.
