    • 3 Days Masai Mara Safaris -$450 PP

    This is a three-day safari tour from Nairobi to Masai Mara and back. The journey includes a full day in the park searching for the Big Five, with overnight stays at your Camp. The package covers park entrance fees, transport, accommodation, meals, bottled water, game drives, and services of a qualified driver guide, but excludes tips and personal expenses.

    Day 1 Nairobi – Masai Mara.

    Get picked up from your Hotel/Residentials and transfer to the office to meet other travelers and depart Nairobi in the morning for a journey south through the floor of the Great Rift Valley.
    Picnic lunch at Narok
    Arrive Mara for late afternoon head for game drives returning back late evening by 6.30pm overnight at your camp.

    Day 2 Masai Mara Full day

    After breakfast get your picnic lunch boxes and head for full day in the park in search of the Big Five.
    Meals and overnight at you camp.

    Day 3 Masai Mara – Nairobi

    After breakfast (Optional visit the Masai Village) depart Mara with picnic lunch at Maimahiu arriving Nairobi late afternoon. App 15:00Hrs
