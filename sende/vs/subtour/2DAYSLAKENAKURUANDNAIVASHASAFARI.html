    • 2 DAYS LAKE NAKURU AND NAIVASHA SAFARI FROM $439PP

    The first stop on this camping safari is Lake Nakuru National Park, located in the Great Rift Valley of Kenya. This park is known for its large population of pink flamingos that gather along the shores of the lake. In addition to flamingos, the park is also home to over 450 species of birds, making it a birdwatcher’s paradise. You may also spot other wildlife such as rhinos, lions, and giraffes during your game drives.

    After exploring Lake Nakuru, the tour continues to Lake Naivasha, the second-largest freshwater lake in Kenya. This lake is a popular spot for bird watching, with over 400 species of birds recorded in the area. You can also take a boat ride on the lake to get up close and personal with hippos and other wildlife. For a unique experience, you can also visit Crescent Island, a private game sanctuary located on the lake.

    One of the highlights of Lake Nakuru and Lake Naivasha Camping Kenya safari is the camping experience. You will stay in tents in designated campsites within the national parks, allowing you to fully immerse yourself in the African wilderness. The campsites are equipped with basic amenities such as toilets and showers, and you will also have access to a campfire where you can gather with your fellow travelers and share stories of your adventures
