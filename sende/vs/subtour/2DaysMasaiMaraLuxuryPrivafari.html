    The Masai Mara is a gem for safari enthusiasts, renowned for the spectacular Great Migration and its impressive array of wildlife, including the iconic Big Five and over 450 bird species. With Namdah Tours, you’ll delve into the heart of this remarkable reserve, experiencing thrilling game drives and unforgettable encounters with nature’s finest.

    After a day filled with exploration and excitement, unwind in comfort at our carefully selected accommodations. Choose from a range of options, including luxury lodges and budget-friendly campsites, all conveniently situated within or near the reserve for easy access to wildlife sightings.

    Dive into the adventure with Namdah Tours and choose the perfect package to suit your budget and preferences:

    • Mid Luxury Package: Experience the magic of the Masai Mara starting from $600 USD per person. Enjoy the exclusivity of a private safari without sharing your safari jeep with other tourists.
    • Luxury Lodge Package: Indulge in luxury amidst the wilderness, with prices starting from $690 USD per person for a private safari experience.

    Day 1, Nairobi City to Masai Mara Game Reserve tour.

    Our expert drivers and staff will ensure a seamless journey, starting with a morning pickup from your hotel or the airport.

    As you depart Nairobi, marvel at the stunning views from the Great Rift Valley escarpment viewpoint, with opportunities for photos and shopping. Then, continue your journey to the iconic Masai Mara Game Reserve, arriving in time for a sumptuous lunch at a luxury lodge.

    After lunch, unwind before embarking on an exhilarating evening game drive, exploring the vast landscapes and spotting the renowned Big Five – Leopard, Lion, Rhino, Elephant, and Buffalo. Encounter a plethora of other African wildlife, including hyenas, giraffes, zebras, and more.
