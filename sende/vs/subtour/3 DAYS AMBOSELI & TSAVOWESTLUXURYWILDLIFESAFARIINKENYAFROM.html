    Day 1 Nairobi City – Amboseli National Park

    Roaming Nomads driver and Staff will pick up the clients on arrival in the airport or hotel in the morning, a short safari briefing will be conducted then the clients depart Nairobi by road and drive through the scenic Athi Plains proceeding into Amboseli National Park. Amboseli is known as the home to the legendary Maasai Warriors and it is famous for huge herds of Elephant, Lions, cheetah, antelope, wildebeest, Giraffe, zebra, Buffalo and also the amazing view of Mount Kilimanjaro. After checking in at the luxury lodge then followed with a hot delicious lunch, after lunch the driver guide and clients will set off for a spectacular and amazing game drive adventure inside Amboseli national park until 6.30pm. Dinner and overnight at the luxury lodge.

    2 Day : Amboseli –Tsavo West National Park

    Second day with Roaming Nomads clients prepare for the early morning game drive adventure inside the Amboseli National park, with the amazing view of the Mount Kilimanjaro, then return to the luxury lodge for full breakfast. Depart Amboseli for Tsavo west National Park arriving in the afternoon, arriving in time for lunch and then proceed for the evening game drive in the park. Dinner and overnight at the luxury lodge

    3 Day : Tsavo West – Nairobi City

    Third day with Roaming Nomads, clients wake up very early in the morning for the game drive inside Tsavo West National park. Tsavo west National park is famous for the Red Elephants, Gerenuk, and Oryx and among other wild animal’s species and bird life. There are other notable features found inside the Tsavo West National park including Mzima Springs which are water source for Mombasa Town, Shetani Lava Flow and Ash cones. Depart Tsavo West National Park for Nairobi, Lunch provided en route arriving in Nairobi in the evening and the driver guide will drop the clients in the hotel or airport and your safari ends with great memories from Roaming Nomads.
