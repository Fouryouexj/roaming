    • 7 DAYS NORTH EAST LAKE TURKANA TOUR

    Explore Northeastern Kenya's beauty with our "7 Days North East Lake Turkana Tour." Travel from Nairobi to Samburu for a game drive, head north to Marsabit for Lake Paradise and community visits, cross the Chalbi Desert to North Horr, and enjoy a day at Lake Turkana. Journey to Maralal via Mallaso Viewpoint before returning to Nairobi, blending adventure, culture, and wildlife for a memorable safari.

    DAY 1: Nairobi- Samburu

    Drive from Nairobi to Samburu 6 hours
    Do an evening game drive in Samburu, Swim in the cooling waters of Buffalo springs
    Visit the Samburu Communities.

    DAY 2 – Samburu- Marsabit

    Drive further North to Marsabit.
    Visit Lake Paradise, Located deep inside Marsabit National Park.
    Visit communities in Marsabit. Visit Hurri hills.
