Exploring Nairobi National Park: A half Day Tour

Nairobi National Park is a must-visit destination for anyone traveling to Kenya. Located just a short drive from the bustling city center, this park offers a unique opportunity to experience a safari without having to travel far from the city.

The main attraction of Nairobi National Park is the game drive. This is where you will have the opportunity to see a variety of wildlife, including lions, giraffes, zebras, and more. The best time for a game drive is early in the morning or late in the afternoon, as this is when the animals are most active. Make sure to bring a camera and binoculars to capture the amazing sights.

The Nairobi National park tour departs daily from Nairobi city at 7.00am until 11.30pm morning tour and 2.00pm until 6.00 pm afternoon tour. Nairobi National Park is unique by being the only protected National park in the world close to a capital city. The park is located In Nairobi city just 7 km from Nairobi city Centre.

The Nairobi National park consist of the savannah ecosystem of different vegetation types. Open grass plains with scattered acacia bush. The western side of the park has a highland dry forest and a permanent river with a riverine forest. To the south of the park are the Athi-Kapiti Plains and Kitengela migration corridor which are important wildlife dispersal areas during the rainy season. Man-made dams within the park have added a further habitat. Major wildlife attractions with the Nairobi National park are the Black rhino, lion, leopard, cheetah, hyena, buffaloes, Giraffe, zebra, wildebeest, elands and diverse birdlife with over 400 species recorded.

A day tour in Nairobi National Park is a perfect way to experience a safari without having to leave the city.
