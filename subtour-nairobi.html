Nairobi National Park Half Day Tour

The safari package offers two options: a morning game drive starting at 6:30 am and ending at 12:30 pm, and an afternoon drive from 2 pm to 6:30 pm, both including hotel pick-up and drop-off. The package covers park entrance fees, transport by safari van, bottled water, game drives, and the services of a qualified driver guide. Excluded from the package are tips, personal items, and any other expenses not listed, such as drinks and souvenirs.

Option 1 -Morning Game drives

Get picked from your hotel by 6am and start game drives at 6.30am until 12.30pm and get dropped back at your hotel.

Option 2 -Afternoon game drives

Get picked from your hotel by 1.30pm and start game drives at 2pm until 6.30pm and get dropped back at your hotel.
