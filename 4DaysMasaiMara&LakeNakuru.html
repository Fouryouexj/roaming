4 Days Masai Mara & Lake Nakuru Budget Safari

This is a four-day safari trip starting from Nairobi to Maasai Mara Game Reserve, then to Lake Nakuru, and back to Nairobi. The trip includes full-day exploration of Maasai Mara, which is part of the Serengeti National Park, and a game drive to Lake Nakuru, known for its flamingoes. The package includes park entrance fees, transport, accommodation, meals, bottled water, game drives, and services of a qualified driver guide, but excludes tips and personal expenses.

Day 1: Nairobi -Masai Mara.

Picked up from your hotel around 7.00am join other travelers and depart Nairobi to Maasai Mara Game Reserve with a stop over at the great rift valley view point.

Day 2: Masai Mara Full day

You arrive masai mara late afternoon, early evening and proceed for a game drive dinner and overnight at your camp.

Day 3: Masai mara/L.Nakuru

After breakfast depart for Lake Nakuru with enroute Picnic lunch at a hotel.
You are going to arrive Nakuru early evening dinner and overnight at a hotel.

Day 4: Day Lake Nakuru/Nairobi

After breakfast proceed for a game drive to Lake Nakuru which is world renowned for its masses of flamingoes and is often referred to as the “Pink Lake”.
Beside birds,it is possible to see small game, like Water Buck, Impala, Rhino and Buffalo in the gardens surrounding.
After lunch depart to Nairobi arriving around 4pm in the evening

PACKAGE EXCLUDES:

  • Tips(Highly Recommended)
  • Items of personal nature
  • All expenses of personal nature (e.g. drinks, laundry, optional activities, souvenirs etc.)
  • Drinks and any other item not specified above.
